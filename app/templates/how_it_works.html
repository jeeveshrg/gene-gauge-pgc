How it works

In four short steps, with no jargon.

  1. 1

    We look at many tiny signals

    A signal is just a small number per person. Each signal on its own says very little. Together, they can hint at a trend.

  2. 2

    We multiply each by how much it counts

    Some signals push a score up. Some push it down. Some barely count at all. Each one has a small weight.

  3. 3

    We add them all up

    The total is your one clear score. A single number you can read and compare.

  4. 4

    We compare with a large group

    We simulate many sample people using the same math and see where your score lands. Lower than most, typical, or higher than most.

What we do not do

  • We do not diagnose anything.
  • We do not use real personal data.
  • We do not sell, share, or store your inputs.
  • We do not pretend this is a medical tool.

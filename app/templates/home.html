GeneGauge

A simple way to turn tiny signals into one clear score.

We look at a list of tiny signals, add them up, and show where your result lands compared with many sample people. No jargon. No guesswork. No medical claims.

Try the calculator How it works

1. Tiny signals

Each person has many small signals. Some push a score up. Some push it down. On their own, none say very much.

2. Simple math

We multiply each signal by how much it counts and add them together. That gives one number: your signal score.

3. Plain comparison

We compare your score with a large group of sample people so you can see where you land - higher, typical, or lower.

What this is - and isn't

GeneGauge is an educational demo. It shows how weighted-sum scoring works in a clean, friendly way. It is not a diagnosis, not medical advice, and uses simulated sample data. If you ever want to see the technical underpinnings, they live in the Details page.
